ViralContent

A private production tool for the YouTube channel Altrabird.

ViralContent makes short, original science videos. Each one asks a single everyday question, such as why ice floats, and answers it with an animation and a narrated explanation. Every fact in a video comes from a cited source.

The tool runs on the channel owner's own computer. It writes and checks each video, uploads it to the Altrabird channel as a private video for the owner to approve, and later reads that channel's own statistics so the owner can see how videos are doing.

Who can use it. Only the owner of the Altrabird channel. ViralContent is not offered to the public, and it never signs in to anyone else's YouTube account.

How it reaches YouTube. Through the official YouTube API Services, with the channel owner's permission.

Read the privacy policy and the terms of service. Questions can be left as an issue at github.com/Altrabird/viralcontent/issues.

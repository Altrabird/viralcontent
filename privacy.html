Privacy policy

ViralContent · Effective 15 November 2026

ViralContent is a private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel Altrabird. You must agree to this policy before using it.

YouTube API Services

ViralContent uses YouTube API Services. When you sign in, Google handles your sign-in and your data under the Google Privacy Policy.

What information it accesses

With your permission, ViralContent accesses:

  • your YouTube channel's name, handle and ID, to make sure it is signed in to the right channel;
  • permission to upload videos to your channel and set their privacy, schedule and audience settings;
  • your channel's statistics and YouTube Analytics reports, such as views and watch time, for your own videos.

It does not access your email, your contacts, your subscriptions, your comments on other channels or any other Google service.

How it is stored and used

Everything ViralContent collects is stored only on the channel owner's own computer. It is used to upload videos and to show the owner how those videos are doing.

The owner may use an AI writing assistant (Claude, made by Anthropic) to plan new videos, and may give it the channel's own statistics for that purpose. Apart from that, information is not shared with anyone, not sold, and not used for advertising.

Statistics are refreshed or deleted on the schedule YouTube's developer policies require.

Cookies and advertising

ViralContent does not place cookies or similar technology on your device or browser, and it does not allow third parties to serve content or advertisements. This website sets no cookies either.

Removing access and deleting data

You can delete ViralContent's stored data by deleting it from the computer it runs on. In addition, you can revoke ViralContent's access to your data at any time from the Google security settings page: security.google.com/settings/security/permissions.

Contact

For questions or complaints about these privacy practices, open an issue at github.com/Altrabird/viralcontent/issues.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted on this page with a new effective date.
